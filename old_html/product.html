The Arch-Series 01 Court Minimaliste strips footwear down to its essential geometry. Cut from Grade-A full-grain Tuscan calfskin that softens progressively with each wear, this silhouette is built around our proprietary zero-drop footbed for natural anatomical stabilization.

  • Tanned in Santa Croce sull'Arno using natural vegetable oils
  • Stitched 360-degree Margom rubber cupsole for decades of wear
  • Reinforced counter with internal micro-suede slip prevention
  • Finished with blind-stamped gold foil Civitanova archive numbering

Engineered with an ergonomically calibrated toe-box that respects the biological splay of the human metatarsals. True to European size standards. If you normally wear a half-size, we recommend calibrating downward to the nearest whole size for optimal heel lock.

Removable Footbed: Includes our molded antimicrobial cork arch insole, easily swappable for bespoke orthotics.

All orders are packaged in recycled rigid unbleached craft boxes with two organic cotton dust bags and an extra set of waxed cotton laces.
